~ OVERVIEW ~

Comprehensive web platform for lodging management, automating room availability, reservation lifecycles, financial controls, and AI-assisted customer service.

~ PLANNING ~

Development based on SCRUM and IEEE 29148:2018 requirements, focusing on eliminating paper records, preventing overbooking, and centralizing administrative workflows.

~ FEATURES ~
  • Interactive real-time calendar and dynamic availability tracking to prevent overbooking.
  • Centralized administrative dashboard with real-time operational metrics and charts.
  • Expense and revenue tracking categorized by type with receipt document uploads.
  • AI-powered conversational chatbot assistant for customer inquiries and management support.
~ IMPACT ~

Replaced manual paper processes with an automated digital flow, eliminating overbooking risks and optimizing administrative efficiency.
