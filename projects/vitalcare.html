~ OVERVIEW ~

Desktop system to optimize medical records, appointments and patient files, centralizing clinical and administrative information.

~ PLANNING ~

Development based on requirements gathered at the University Hospital to prioritize critical functions and reduce bottlenecks.

~ FEATURES ~
  • Medical appointment management with a calendar view.
  • File viewer for quick access to historical patient documentation.
  • Advanced search with smart filters for rapid record retrieval.
  • Admin dashboard featuring real-time operational metrics.
~ IMPACT ~

Replaced paper processes with digital flows, reducing response times and minimizing errors.
